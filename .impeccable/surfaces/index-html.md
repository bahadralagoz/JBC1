# Surface brief: storefront home (index.html)

Scope: JBC brand home + storefront prototype. Mode: Persuade.
Audience/job: men arriving from Trendyol or social; pick a piece, colour and size, add to cart.
Proof/content: four product lines (polo, t-shirt, sweatshirt, shorts). Real photos and logo exist but are not in the repo; all names, prices, fabric specs are labelled samples.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, portable to a commerce platform later; checkout not wired.
Unresolved: real prices, fabrics, colourways, photography, logo file, positioning.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shirt is launched like a car: one piece on a showroom turntable, configured live. Refuses the fashion default of a full-bleed campaign slider over a grid of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Pale studio-grey showroom that is drenched in the selected colourway; expanded heavy grotesk (Archivo, wide axis) for model names, tabular numerals for specs; hairline spec rules, a lit elliptical turntable, round paint-chip swatches, rectangular trim-selector controls.

STORY: Visitor sees one JBC piece presented with weight, understands JBC makes its own basics, changes colour and sees the whole room change, picks a size, adds to cart; then compares the four models in a spec sheet and finds the Trendyol store.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Giant expanded model name across the back wall behind the garment; garment centred on the turntable at ~55% height; right column: model selector, colour chips, size grid, price, primary "Sepete ekle"; spec strip along the bottom edge.

FORM: Automotive configurator / launch showroom, candidate 2 of the re-rolled list (user's choice). seed:1f737175cb44a1b4

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
