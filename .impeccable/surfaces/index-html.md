# Surface brief: storefront home (index.html)

Scope: JBC brand home + storefront prototype. Mode: Persuade.
Audience/job: men arriving from Trendyol or social; pick a piece, colour and size, add to cart.
Proof/content: six models from the Trendyol export (Sweatshirt, Eşofman Takımı, Kapüşonlu, Oversize Tişört, Tişört, Şort), JBC's own cut-out product photos, Trendyol prices and ratings, a Trendyol link per colourway. No polo (not in the export). Sizes are a labelled placeholder.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, portable to a commerce platform later; checkout not wired.
Unresolved: size/stock data, logo file, positioning, commerce platform. The "Koleksiyon" tile row was added during the build and awaits the user's sign-off (remove it if declined).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shirt is launched like a car: one piece on a showroom turntable, configured live. Refuses the fashion default of a full-bleed campaign slider over a grid of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: A showroom drenched in the selected colourway, the real cut-out product photo standing on a lit elliptical turntable; pale studio-grey paper for the sections below; expanded heavy grotesk (Archivo, wide axis) for model names, tabular numerals for specs; hairline spec rules, round paint-chip swatches (a dot marks logo/detail variants), rectangular trim-selector controls.

STORY: Visitor sees one JBC piece presented with weight, understands JBC makes its own basics, changes colour and sees the whole room change, picks a size, adds to cart; then compares the four models in a spec sheet and finds the Trendyol store.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Giant expanded model name across the back wall behind the figure; the cut-out photo centred on the turntable (half-body shots fade into the disc, shorts start below the lettering); right column: product name as heading, model selector, colour chips, size grid with stock note, Trendyol price and rating, primary "Sepete ekle", Trendyol link; spec strip along the bottom edge.
